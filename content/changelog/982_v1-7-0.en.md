## Added
- Vulnerabilities can be [filtered](/client/consumer/vulnerabilities/#triage) by severity.
- During [CSAF Generation](/client/manager/doc-management/csaf-gen/) a CPE or pURL can now be provided for a product. Furthermore it can be prevented that a "vendor" entry is created inside the product tree.

## Changed
- Improved matching algorithm between SBOMs and CSAFs.

## Fixed
- Duplicated component in a BOM are now treated as a warning instead of an error, since they may appear in the syft output.
